# Deficiency is an association, not proof of causation

Epidemiological links between low 25(OH)D and respiratory infections are
frequently confounded: sick and winter housebound people have low sun exposure,
deficient diet, and more infections. A meta-analysis of mendelian-randomisation
studies found no evidence that genetically-lowered vitamin D causes more
respiratory infections. Serum level is a marker, not a proven cause.
