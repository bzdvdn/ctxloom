# Cohort study: vitamin D and winter colds

A cohort of 19,000 adults was followed through two winter seasons. Participants
with serum 25(OH)D above 30 ng/ml had 28% fewer documented acute respiratory
infections compared with the deficient group, after adjusting for age,
smoking and indoor crowding. The authors conclude that maintaining adequate
vitamin D status is associated with a lower incidence of colds.
