# Invoices and payment

Payment runs on a subscription with a monthly cycle. Every month an invoice is
generated: the base part by plan plus the actual resource usage.

## Invoicing

- on the first day of the month an invoice is generated for the past period;
- the invoice is available in the "Billing" section in PDF format;
- line-item details show each position.

## Payment methods

Bank cards, automatic payment from the bank account, and invoice payment for
legal entities are accepted. Legal entity details change through support.

## Autopay

Autopay charges the amount on the invoice day. If funds are insufficient, a
notification arrives; access is kept for 7 more days and then suspended. A
suspended account is not deleted — data remains available after payment.

## VAT

For residents, VAT is included in the invoice amount and shown as a separate
line. Required documents (invoice, acceptance certificate) are generated
automatically in the "Documents" section.

## Changing the plan

A plan change applies from the next billing period. Overpayment for unused
days is refunded as a balance on the next invoice.

## Refunds

For technical problems on our side, a refund is processed on a support request
within 30 days. On request, a refund is processed manually.
